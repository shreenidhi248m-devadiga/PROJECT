Engineered for Academic Excellence & Transparency

Every component of our system is crafted to eliminate manual paperwork, enforce compliance with university rules, and support student success.

Attendance Management

Automated session-wise and lecture-wise logging with biometric sync. Automatically flags students below 75% for mandatory condonation review.

  • 1-click batch attendance marking
  • Real-time absentee SMS/push alerts
  • University exam eligibility reports

Student Information System

Centralized profile repository keeping track of academic history, roll numbers, semester GPAs (SGPA), internal assessment marks, and contact records.

  • Complete SGPA & mark trajectories
  • Instant digital student verification
  • Secure parent contact synchronization

Faculty Administration

Faculty profile directories with department designations, academic qualifications, lecture schedules, and direct subject performance insights.

  • HOD-level department oversight
  • Syllabus coverage tracking
  • Subject performance analytics

Grievance & Complaints Cell

Transparent student redressal portal. Students file complaints categorized by Library, Infrastructure, or Canteen, with live tracking till resolution.

  • Pending, In Review, Resolved status
  • Direct administrator escalation
  • Verified closure documentation

Achievements & Laurels

Showcase outstanding accomplishments in Smart India Hackathon, state-level project expos, university ranks, and inter-collegiate competitions.

  • National & State level award logs
  • Student digital certificates
  • Annual college honors gallery

Statistical Intelligence

Data-backed visualization of campus performance: interactive line charts for department growth, pass percentages, and 1st, 2nd, and 3rd year distributions.

  • Department comparative line graphs
  • Class-wise academic breakdown
  • Exportable compliance data
